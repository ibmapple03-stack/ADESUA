About ADA

A servant leader.
A man of God. A man of the people.

Apostle Ayodele David-Adesua — popularly known as ADA, Imole Ekiti — has spent his life in quiet, consistent service, long before politics called him forward. Born on 13 December 1973 and raised in Ijesa Isu-Ekiti, his journey has been shaped by faith, community and a deep belief in the dignity of every person.

His mission is simple and direct: to import the dividends of democracy into Ekiti North Federal Constituency 1, for the benefit of all. Not to represent himself — but to represent the people of Ikole and Oye in Abuja.

“

For too long our voice has been muffled in Abuja. I am not coming to represent myself, I am coming to represent YOU. This is my covenant with you — issue by issue, we will rebuild Ekiti Oke together.

— Apostle Ayodele David-Adesua, ADA · Imole Ekiti

Our Vision

The 6-Point Agenda for Ekiti North 1

Practical, people-centred plans built around the real problems of Ikole and Oye — not empty promises.

01

Education

"Light for Every Child"

Our schools need infrastructure, teachers, and modern learning tools.

  • ●Federal-backed renovation of primary & secondary schools
  • ●Scholarships & bursaries for indigent students
  • ●ICT/computer centers in every major town

“An educated Ekiti Oke is an empowered Ekiti Oke.”

02

Job Creation & Empowerment

"Work for Our Youth"

Jobs are scarce. Our graduates are roaming.

  • ●Federal job slots & employment opportunities
  • ●SME grants & soft loans with BOI, NDE, CBN
  • ●Skill acquisition centers in Ikole & Oye

“When our youth work, Ekiti Oke works.”

03

Roads & Infrastructure

"Opening Ekiti Oke"

Bad roads are killing our farmers and traders.

  • ●Lobby FERMA for key inter-community roads
  • ●Rural roads connecting farms to markets
  • ●Solar street lights in markets & junctions

“Good roads bring food to the table.”

04

Health

Accessible care for every home

Our people travel far for basic healthcare. Clinics lack drugs, equipment and personnel.

  • ●Upgraded Primary Health Centres
  • ●Equipped hospitals & trained personnel
  • ●Health insurance awareness for the vulnerable

“A healthy people is a productive people.”

05

Agriculture & Food Security

From our land, our future

Our farmers have the land and the zeal, but lack support, storage and market access.

  • ●Inputs, training & cooperative financing
  • ●Storage facilities to cut post-harvest losses
  • ●Market linkages for better income

“When we feed our people, we secure our future.”

06

Women, Youth & Vulnerable

Investing in people

Our women and youth are full of potential but lack skills, capital and opportunities.

  • ●Skills acquisition, grants & startup support
  • ●Special programs for persons with disabilities
  • ●Support for the vulnerable in our constituency

“Leaders build people.”
